Practice One of Two
Actuarial · Strategy

The work Chuck does personally.

Chuck Ritzke has spent four decades at the intersection of insurance product development and actuarial rigor. The work in this practice isn't delegated — it's the work Chuck does himself. From initial product concept through regulatory approval, every engagement here is led by an FSA with the depth to identify risk before it becomes a problem and the creativity to design products that work in the real world. This is not boilerplate consulting. The products that come out of this practice are built on 40 years of doing the hard work no one else will touch.

PSE's actuarial strategy work spans the full product lifecycle — from early-stage feasibility and pricing through ongoing actuarial support, software development, and carrier negotiations. Whether you're a carrier launching a new product line, a distributor bringing a concept to market, or an insurtech company building credibility with a carrier partner, this is where that work gets done right.

Actuarial Consulting

FSA-level actuarial work across the full range of life, annuity, and specialty insurance products. Chuck brings 40 years of practical application — not theoretical frameworks, but the kind of actuarial judgment that comes from having done this work for real carriers and real products.

  • Pricing, valuation, and reserve analysis
  • Risk assessment and stress testing
  • Financial modeling and projections
  • Actuarial opinion and sign-off
Product Design & Pricing

From concept to filing-ready product. Chuck has designed products across life, annuity, worksite, and specialty lines — including two patented innovations developed in partnership with carrier clients. The goal is always a product that works actuarially, passes regulatory scrutiny, and sells.

  • New product concept development and feasibility
  • Market and competitive analysis
  • Filing-ready actuarial memoranda
  • Post-launch monitoring and experience studies
Software Development

Custom actuarial and insurance software built for problems that off-the-shelf tools can't solve. Illustration engines, planning platforms, administrative tools, and data systems — designed by people who understand both the insurance problem and the technical requirements.

  • Illustration and quoting systems
  • Financial planning and modeling platforms
  • Data analysis and regulatory reporting tools
  • API integration and system interfaces
Insurtech Advising

Strategic actuarial guidance for technology companies operating in the insurance space. Chuck has worked with insurtechs from seed stage through IPO — providing the actuarial credibility and carrier-facing expertise that most technology companies don't have internally and can't easily hire.

  • Actuarial credibility and carrier negotiation support
  • Product structure and insurance risk assessment
  • Regulatory strategy and filing guidance
  • SOA insurtech mentor program participant
Who this is for

Actuarial strategy clients come in three forms.

Carriers

Carriers working on new product development, legacy product modernization, or entering a new line of business. Chuck has been the actuarial lead on product launches for major carriers and first-time market entrants alike — and has the credibility and track record to navigate the most complex regulatory environments.

Distributors & IMOs

Distribution organizations with a product idea that needs actuarial development and carrier backing to become real. We do the technical work that makes a concept viable — pricing, structuring, and filing — and bring the carrier relationships necessary to move from concept to market.

Insurtech Companies

Technology companies operating in the insurance space who need actuarial credibility, carrier access, or the ability to have a technically serious conversation with an underwriter. We've supported founders from seed stage through public offering, and we understand the pace at which these companies need to operate.

Practice Two of Two
Operations · Support

The execution layer that makes innovation real.

Not every piece of work in insurance is glamorous. System conversions, compliance filings, legacy block administration, ongoing policy support — this is the operational layer that holds insurance products together after they're launched. Without it, even the best product design falls apart in execution. PSE's administrative and operational practice handles this work with the same precision and institutional knowledge we bring to product development.

This practice is team-delivered. Chuck has built a team of specialists with deep experience in insurance operations, regulatory compliance, and administrative systems. They handle the work that's too important to hand off to someone who doesn't understand insurance, and too time-consuming to staff internally. If you've worked with PSE on a product launch, this is the team that keeps it running.

Who this is for

Operational work serves all three audiences differently.

Carriers

Carriers with in-force blocks that need ongoing administration, multi-state compliance filing needs, or operational complexity stemming from legacy systems and discontinued products. We've been in the engine room with carriers through system conversions, regulatory overhauls, and decades-old closed block issues. We know how this work gets done.

Distributors & IMOs

Distribution organizations that need ongoing operational support for products already in the market — illustration infrastructure, compliance coordination across states, or marketing and sales support materials. Most relevant as a follow-on to a product development engagement, though we take operational-only engagements as well.

Insurtech Companies

Insurtech companies managing the operational reality of an insurance product in the market. Most technology companies aren't built to handle the compliance, administration, and reporting work that comes with a live insurance product. We handle that layer so your team can focus on what it's actually built to do.
