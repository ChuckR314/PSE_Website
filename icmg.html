The Organization

What ICMG is.

ICMG is a non-profit association built around a straightforward observation: most meaningful growth in insurance and financial services happens through partnerships, and partnerships happen between people who already know each other. It has been doing that work for more than forty years.

Its members are the people who make those deals — executives and business development leaders at insurance carriers, distributors and marketing organizations, third-party administrators, and the companies that support the life, health, and Medicare markets. They join to meet counterparts they wouldn't otherwise cross paths with, to compare notes on what's actually working, and to find partners for new products and new distribution.

It is a deliberately small community for an industry this size. That's the point. Members who show up consistently become known quantities, and the introductions start coming to them.

Membership

Why it's worth joining.

If your growth depends on partnerships, ICMG is the shortest path to the people who can approve them.

The room is decision-makers

Members are senior enough to say yes. You aren't spending two years working your way up to the person who can actually do the deal — they're already at the table.

The format is built for connecting

Member introductions, scheduled one-on-one meetings, and a networking hall designed so you can find specific people instead of wandering past booths hoping to get lucky.

Relationships compound

Members who keep showing up build a network that pays off for years. The people you meet in your first year are the ones you call in your fifth.

You'll be known

A community of a few hundred is small enough to have a reputation in. Attend consistently and people know your name, your company, and what you're looking for.

The Annual Conference

One event a year. It's the one that counts.

Three days of getting the right people in front of each other.

The ICMG Annual Conference is where the year's introductions get made. Attendees deliver short opening introductions to the full room, book one-on-one meetings in advance, work the networking hall, and sit in on sessions covering what's moving in the life, health, and Medicare markets.

It has a reputation among people who've attended for years as the best networking conference in the industry. That reputation is the reason PSE keeps going back.
